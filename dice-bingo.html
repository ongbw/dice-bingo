Dice Bingo Solver

David J. Hemmer and Benjamin W. Ong — Department of Mathematical Sciences, Michigan Technological University

Each player fills a board with dice sums (2–12). On each turn a pair of dice is rolled; players mark a matching cell on their board. The first to complete a row, column, or main diagonal wins. This tool computes optimal play via exact backward induction.
